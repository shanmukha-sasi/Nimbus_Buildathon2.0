ABOUT THE HACKATHON

Fueling Innovation & Solutions

A high-impact product sprint where student developers apply modern software development, cloud infrastructure (AWS), and Artificial Intelligence to solve complex, real-world problems.

Dates

September 15 - 20

Save the dates for this intensive, two-day collaborative hackathon journey.

Problem Statements

Released 1 Week Prior

Themes and problem statements will be released one week early so teams can brainstorm and formulate conceptual designs.

Building Sprint

24–48 Hours

Continuous coding, designing, Cloud architecture setup, and final product deployment.

Team Structure

2–3 Members

Collaborate and build with your friends. Individual registrations will not be allowed.

About the Sponsor

Title Sponsor & Partner

Frontlines Media (FLM)

Empowering Learners & Bridging Upskilling Gaps

Frontlines Media (operating under Frontlines EduTech Private Limited or FLM) is a prominent Hyderabad-based e-learning and career guidance platform. Founded in 2021 and led by CEO Krishna Mantravadi, the startup focuses heavily on making technical upskilling, mock testing, and job notifications accessible to young professionals and students, with a strong emphasis on empowering learners across the Telugu states and rural regions.
